What to Pack for Santorini in Summer

Santorini in summer is all blue domes, white steps, and long golden evenings over the caldera. The days are hot and bright, the sea is the whole point, and the villages ask for something that looks effortless in every photo. Pack light, pack breezy, and leave room for the light layer you'll want at sunset.

Carry-on friendlyJun–Sep4 min read

The weather at a glance

Daytime
Hot and dry, 79–88°F (26–31°C), intense sun
Evenings
Warm but breezy — the meltemi wind picks up after sunset
Rain
Almost none in high summer
The move
Breathable dresses, swimwear on rotation, sandals that handle stone steps

The everyday sundress

A flowy cotton or linen sundress is the workhorse of a Santorini wardrobe. It reads polished against the whitewashed walls, moves in the breeze, and takes you from a morning coffee in Oia to lunch by the water without a thought. Two or three in light, warm-weather colours cover most of the trip.

Swimwear on rotation

Between hotel plunge pools, black-sand beaches, and boat days around the caldera, you'll be in swimwear more than out of it. Two suits let one dry while you wear the other — a job the salt air does slowly.

Sandals for the steps

Santorini is steep and cobbled — the postcard villages are basically staircases. Bring one flat leather sandal that handles a day of walking, plus one dressier pair for dinner. Skip the delicate heels; the stone steps will win.

A light layer for the wind

Once the sun drops, the meltemi breeze makes the caldera evenings genuinely cool. A light linen shirt or a fine knit thrown over a dress is all you need.

The one-week Santorini capsule

  • 2–3 breezy sundresses (light, warm-weather colours)
  • 1 maxi or slip dress for caldera dinners
  • 2 swimsuits
  • 1 kaftan or beach coverup
  • 1 flat leather sandal + 1 dressier pair
  • 1 wide-brim straw hat + sunglasses
  • 1 roomy straw tote
  • 1 light linen shirt or fine knit for breezy evenings
  • Underwear, sleepwear, and reef-friendly sunscreen
