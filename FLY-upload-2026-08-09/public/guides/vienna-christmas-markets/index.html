What to Pack for Vienna's Christmas Markets

Vienna in December is pure winter magic — imperial squares strung with lights, mulled wine, and some of Europe's most beautiful Christkindlmärkte. But this is genuine cold, and you'll be standing outdoors for hours. How you pack decides everything.

Cold-weatherLate Nov–Dec4 min read

The weather at a glance

Daytime
35–43°F (2–6°C), often grey
Evenings
28–36°F (-2 to 2°C), sometimes below freezing
Conditions
Cold, damp, occasional snow; wind bites harder
The move
Thermal layers, a properly warm coat, warm extremities

The base layer

Warmth starts against the skin. A thermal or merino base layer is the secret to staying out for hours without shivering — thin enough to wear under everything, warm out of proportion to its weight. Pack two so one is always fresh.

The essential: a properly warm coat

This is the item that makes or breaks the trip. You want a genuinely insulated coat — a wool overcoat with weight to it, or a refined puffer that reads elegant enough for the Staatsoper.

The accessories that actually matter

In this cold, accessories aren't optional — they're the difference-maker. A warm hat, a good scarf, and insulated gloves let you linger at the markets instead of retreating indoors.

The Vienna Christmas-market capsule

  • 2 thermal/merino base-layer tops
  • 1–2 warm sweaters
  • 1 properly warm coat (wool or insulated puffer)
  • 1 pair warm trousers + thermal tights
  • 1 pair insulated, water-resistant boots
  • Hat, scarf, gloves
  • Wool socks
  • Underwear, sleepwear
