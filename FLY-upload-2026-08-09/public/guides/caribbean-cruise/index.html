What to Pack for a Caribbean Cruise or Resort

Warm water, white sand, and a rhythm of beach days, island excursions, and dinners that range from barefoot to dressed-up. A Caribbean cruise or resort trip is really two wardrobes in one bag — daytime swimwear on rotation, and a couple of evening looks for the nights the dress code steps up. Here's how to cover both without overpacking.

The weather at a glance

Daytime
Hot and humid, 82–88°F (28–31°C), strong sun
Evenings
Warm — but ship dining rooms and A/C run cold
Rain
Short tropical showers, often over in minutes
The move
Swimwear on rotation, breezy dresses, one or two dressier looks

Swimwear on rotation

This is the trip you actually live in swimwear — pool deck, beach stops, and excursions back to back. Three suits mean you're never reaching for a damp one, and you can mix a bright bikini day with a sleek one-piece for the pool bar.

Coverups for deck to lunch

A kaftan or sarong turns a swimsuit into a lunch outfit in seconds — essential on a cruise where the buffet is a wet-swimsuit no-go. Pack two light ones; they weigh nothing.

Breezy day dresses

For port days, shopping, and exploring, a couple of light cotton or linen dresses keep you cool and cover you up when an excursion calls for it. They double as easy dinner options on casual nights.

The formal-night look

Most cruises have one or two "elegant" or formal evenings, and resorts have their dressier restaurants too. One dressy maxi or cocktail dress you feel wonderful in covers every one of them — pick a colour you love in photos.

Sandals for sand and deck

You need one pair of flat sandals or flip-flops for the beach and pool, and one dressier sandal for dinner. A low block heel is safer than a stiletto on a moving deck.

A light layer for the A/C

Cruise dining rooms, theatres, and resort restaurants run surprisingly cold. A light wrap or cardigan you can fold into a bag saves the evening — and doubles as sun cover on deck.

Beach bag and sun protection

A roomy waterproof-lined tote carries the beach day, and a hat plus sunglasses and reef-safe sunscreen protect you through hours of exposed sun and salt.

The one-week cruise & resort capsule

  • 3 swimsuits
  • 2 kaftans or sarong coverups
  • 2 breezy day dresses
  • 1 dressy maxi or cocktail dress (formal night)
  • 1 flat beach sandal + 1 dressy block-heel sandal
  • 1 packable wrap or light cardigan
  • 1 roomy beach tote
  • Sun hat, sunglasses, reef-safe sunscreen
  • Underwear, sleepwear, and a light rain shell for excursions
