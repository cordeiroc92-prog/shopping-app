Affiliate disclosure

Feel Like You is free to use because of affiliate commissions. This page explains, in plain language, what that means for you.

The short version

Some of the product links on Feel Like You are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer. It costs you nothing extra — the price is the same whether you use our link or go to the store directly.

How it works

When we recommend a piece of clothing, we link to a retailer through an affiliate network. If your purchase qualifies, the retailer pays us a small percentage of the sale. That commission is what keeps FLY free and lets us build features like weather-matched packing lists and price tracking.

We participate in affiliate and retailer programs through third-party networks. Not every link on the site earns a commission; where a link isn't part of a program, it simply points you to where the item can be found.

Our recommendations are not for sale

This is the important part: retailers cannot pay us to rank a product higher. What FLY suggests is driven by your taste and the forecast for your trip — not by which link pays the most. An affiliate relationship never changes what we recommend or the order we recommend it in.

Where you'll see this

You'll find a short reminder near shoppable links throughout the app and our packing guides, so it's always clear when a link may earn us a commission. This page is the fuller version of that reminder.

FTC note

In line with U.S. Federal Trade Commission guidelines on endorsements and affiliate relationships, we disclose that Feel Like You has financial relationships with the retailers and networks behind some of the links on this site.
