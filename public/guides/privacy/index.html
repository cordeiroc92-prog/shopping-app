Privacy policy

This policy explains what Feel Like You (FLY) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. In plain terms: we collect the little we need to make the app work, and we don't sell your data.

What we collect

If you sign in, we collect the email address you give us. As you use FLY we also store the choices you make in the app itself: items you like or skip, your closet and how many of each thing you own, trips you plan, and products you watch. Much of this is stored locally in your browser. None of it requires a photo of you or your belongings.

How we use it

Your likes and closet build the taste and packing signal that powers recommendations, so the app can tell you what a trip needs and what you're missing. Your email lets us keep your account across visits. We don't sell any of this.

Affiliate links

Product links in FLY are affiliate links. If you buy something after clicking one, we may earn a small commission from the retailer. It costs you nothing extra, and it's how FLY stays free to use. See our affiliate disclosure for the full explanation.

Cookies & local storage

FLY uses your browser's local storage to remember your session, your taste profile, and your closet between visits. Affiliate networks and our hosting and analytics providers may set cookies needed to run the service and to measure which features are used. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

Third parties

We share the minimum needed to run the app: affiliate networks to track qualifying purchases, and standard hosting and analytics providers to keep FLY running and to see which features are actually used. We don't sell personal data to advertisers.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it at any time. Reach out using the contact details below and we'll take care of it. You can also clear locally stored data yourself by clearing site data in your browser.

Children

FLY isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and post the new version here.

Contact

Questions or a deletion request go to hello@shopfeellikeyou.com, or see our contact page.
