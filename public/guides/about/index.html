About

Style that's actually yours.

Feel Like You (FLY) is a style-discovery and trip-packing app. It learns your taste, keeps track of what you already own, and turns every trip into a packing list built from the real forecast at each stop — so you know what's missing before you go.

What FLY does

Most of us shop for a trip twice: once in a panic before we leave, and again when we get there and realize we forgot something. FLY is built to end that. You swipe through pieces so the app can learn what you actually like, you tell it what's already in your closet, and when you plan a trip it checks the weather at every stop and shows you what to pack and what you're missing.

When a recommendation is something worth buying, we point you to the retailer that sells it. Those product links are affiliate links, which is how the app stays free to use. We explain exactly how that works on our affiliate disclosure page.

How it works

Discover is a swipe deck ranked to your taste — every like refines what shows up next. Your closet is a one-time setup you reuse on every trip. Trip planning builds an itinerary and pulls the forecast for each destination, then generates a weather-matched packing list. Price watching lets you track pieces you're eyeing and tells you when the price actually drops.

Who we are

Feel Like You is an independent project run by a small team. We're not owned by any retailer, and we don't take payment to rank one product above another — recommendations come from your taste and the weather, not from who pays us. If you want to reach a real person, our contact page has the details.
