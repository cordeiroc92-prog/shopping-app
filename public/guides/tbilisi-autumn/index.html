What to Pack for Tbilisi in Autumn

Tbilisi ranked as the world's #2 trending destination for 2026, and autumn is when Georgia's capital shines — wine-harvest days, golden light on balconied old-town houses, and crisp evenings made for long supra feasts. Expect dramatic swings between afternoon and night.

Carry-on friendlySept–Nov4 min read

The weather at a glance

Early autumn
Warm, 72–80°F (22–27°C), sunny
Late autumn
Cooler, 48–63°F (9–17°C), rising rain
Evenings
Drop sharply after sunset — a real layer needed
The move
Versatile neutral layers, a warm knit, a weatherproof jacket, cobblestone-ready shoes

The layering foundation

Warm afternoons and cool mornings mean breathable tops you can build on. Clean, simple pieces in neutral tones work across the whole trip — three or four in muted shades will mix into a full week of looks.

The one-week Tbilisi capsule

  • 3–4 lightweight tops (muted neutrals)
  • 1 mid-weight knit sweater
  • 1 water-resistant jacket or trench
  • 1 pair comfortable trousers
  • 1 pair jeans
  • 1 comfortable walking shoe
  • 1 crossbody bag
  • Underwear, socks, sleepwear
  • A compact umbrella for late-autumn showers
