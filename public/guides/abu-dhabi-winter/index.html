What to Pack for Abu Dhabi in Winter

Winter is the only sensible time to visit Abu Dhabi, and it's glorious — warm, sunny days and balmy evenings from November through March. The packing challenge is twofold: dress for genuine warmth, but pack respectfully for cultural and religious sites.

Carry-on friendlyNov–Mar4 min read

The weather at a glance

Daytime
75–82°F (24–28°C), sunny and dry
Evenings
60–66°F (15–19°C) — occasionally needs a light layer
Cultural sites
Shoulders and knees covered; a head scarf for mosques
The move
Breathable warm-weather pieces that also cover up easily

The daytime foundation

Light, breathable fabrics in loose cuts keep you cool and double as modest coverage. Lightweight long sleeves are your friend — cooler in strong sun than you'd expect and respectful at any site. Neutral, airy pieces in linen or cotton work everywhere from the beach club to the mosque.

The one-week Abu Dhabi capsule

  • 3–4 loose breathable tops (include long sleeves)
  • 1 lightweight cover-up / overshirt
  • 1 fine knit cardigan for evenings and AC
  • 1 pair wide-leg linen trousers
  • 1 maxi skirt or second relaxed bottom
  • 1 large lightweight scarf
  • Walking sandals + 1 closed evening shoe
  • Crossbody bag
  • Swimwear + a cover-up
  • Underwear, sleepwear, sun protection
